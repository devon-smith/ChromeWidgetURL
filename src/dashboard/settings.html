New tab page

Local Toby is your new tab page — every new tab opens this dashboard, like the old Toby. The current-window tabs bar (toggle above) lets you drop what you have open into a collection in one click. To turn the takeover off, remove the chrome_url_overrides block from manifest.json and reload the extension; the dashboard still opens with Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+O.

Danger zone
